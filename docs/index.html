Support for BockFind

BockFind is a word-search puzzle app that challenges players to find hidden words inside a randomized letter grid before time runs out.

Randomized letter grid Timed rounds Multiple difficulty levels

How BockFind Works

  • Start a round and BockFind generates a fresh randomized letter grid.
  • Find as many hidden words as possible before the timer expires.
  • Tap letters in sequence to spell out words — words can run in any direction.
  • Score points for every word you find; longer words earn more points.
  • Adjust difficulty and timer length in Settings to match your skill level.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I reset my progress? — Open Settings and select Reset Game Data.
  • The app crashed — what do I do? — Force-quit and relaunch BockFind. If the issue persists, contact support.
  • How do I change the timer length? — Visit Settings to adjust round duration.
  • Is an internet connection required? — No, BockFind works fully offline.
