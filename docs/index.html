Support for BockFind

BockFind is a word-search puzzle app that challenges players to find hidden words inside a randomized letter grid before time runs out.

Randomized letter grid Timed rounds Multiple difficulty levels

Overview

  • Create a new puzzle by choosing your preferred difficulty and grid size.
  • BockFind generates a seeded letter grid, and you can share that exact puzzle with a code.
  • Drag across letters to select words; selection snaps to valid directions for the difficulty.
  • Each found word adds to your score, and elapsed time is tracked during play.
  • The puzzle is complete when every word in the list has been found.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I play the same puzzle as someone else? — Paste their puzzle code on the home screen, then tap Play matching puzzle.
  • Can I change difficulty and board size? — Yes. Use the Grid and Difficulty tiles on the home screen before creating a puzzle.
  • Can I continue a puzzle later? — If you still have an active puzzle, use Return to current puzzle from the home screen.
  • The app crashed — what do I do? — Force-quit and relaunch BockFind. If the issue persists, contact support.
